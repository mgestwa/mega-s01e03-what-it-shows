What needs fixing

How to triage the next report

Decision 0001 tells the desk to merge on normalized subject lines. Check the cause first. Two reports with the same subject from the same day-first booking-v3 tenant can need different fixes if one came through the form and the other through the app. An app report and an email report can share the parser cause even when their subjects differ.

  1. Look up the tenant's date order and form version, then identify the report's channel.
